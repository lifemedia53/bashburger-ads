📌 POST 2 – How Do Clogged Gutters Affect Your Huntsville Home?

Fecha publicación interna: 23 noviembre 2026

Clogged gutters can send rainwater spilling over the edge instead of away from your home. That water can stain siding, soak the soil near the foundation, and leave damp spots around the roofline.

Fall is when this problem shows up in Huntsville, since leaves and debris pile up in gutters after the trees drop their leaves. Once the rain returns, a blocked gutter has nowhere to send the water.

Pressures Perfect is a veteran-owned company, and our Gutter Cleaning service covers homes in Huntsville and Athens. Here are signs it may be time to call:

- Water pouring over the edge of the gutter during a storm
- Dark streaks on the siding right below the gutter line
- Plants or small sprouts growing out of the gutters
- Drooping or pulling sections along the roofline
